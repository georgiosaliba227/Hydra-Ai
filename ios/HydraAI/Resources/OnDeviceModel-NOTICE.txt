Modifications: Hydra froze the pretrained image backbone, precomputed its
equivalent 224px position embeddings, trained a small category head, and
converted the inference graph to a fixed-resolution FP16 Core ML model.
This is a separate compact model, not the trained 30B Mac adapter.

Training data: the project's existing reviewed 250-photo Open Food Facts
cohort; only the 150-photo train split fits the head. Public image attribution,
source URLs, hashes and licenses are recorded in the repository at:
ai/beverage_training/manifests/beverage-250-20261102/manifest.jsonl
Images are CC-BY-SA-3.0; source catalog metadata is ODbL-1.0.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
No training photos are bundled with the app. No personal camera or health
records were used to train this model.

Limits: experimental beverage-category suggestions, uncalibrated scores,
small mostly packaged-beverage cohort and no validated open-world rejection.
Printed-size reading is separate local Apple Vision OCR with deterministic
unit validation. The model does not measure remaining liquid, actual
consumption, nutritional composition or physiological hydration.
